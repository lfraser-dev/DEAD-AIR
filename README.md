# DEAD AIR

A third-person helicopter shooter in an open world full of zombies. Plain JavaScript modules and WebGL2, no build step: open `index.html` from any web server.

**The published copy is locked, like The Semi-Finals.** `docs/` is what GitHub Pages serves (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, folder `/docs`): it looks like GitHub's "There isn't a GitHub Pages site here." page. Type the secret word anywhere (on a phone, tap the grey logo five times) and the password screen fades in; the password unscrambles the game (`docs/g.bin`, AES-256-GCM, key from PBKDF2). Same word and password as The Semi-Finals. A device that's unlocked once goes straight in (add `?lock` to forget). After changing the game, rebuild it with `python3 tools/seal.py` (reads the git-ignored `.sealpass` and `.sealword`).

**Flying**: three styles, picked in the hangar. EASY (the default): the mouse steers (left/right turns, forward/back flies forward/back), A / D slide sideways, W / S up and down, it hovers when you let go, crashes hurt less and the gun helps you aim. ASSIST and PRO fly like Battlefield 2042 (mouse tilts, A / D turn; PRO has no levelling). Click: nose gun · right click: rockets · Space: bombs · Shift: afterburner · V: siren (with the loudspeaker) · C: camera.

**The world**: 9.6 km across and made from a seed, so every NEW WORLD (in the hangar) is different, and friends who join get the same one. Mountain ranges with snow on top, rivers winding through valleys, lakes, forests of thousands of trees (you can crash into them), farmland, about 60 farms, 40 named towns and villages, three small cities with proper street grids, and THE CITY: an island 2 km long between two wide rivers that wind off across the country, laid out like Manhattan, with skyscrapers up to 300 m in Downtown and Midtown, a big park with a lake, Times Square neon, jammed avenues, walk-ups with fire escapes, ships in the river, and two huge suspension bridges (one to THE BOROUGH, rows of brownstones with schools, churches and suburbs across the east river), a stone seawall with piers, pier sheds and boats, and docks with container cranes, oil tanks and a rail yard past the top of the island. Press M for the map (shaded hills, rivers, every town named). Out in the country: FORT CALDER (a walled army base with barracks, an armoury, hangars and watchtowers), ST MARY'S HOSPITAL and three RADIO MASTS on the hilltops. Your base is THE SPIRE: a tower in Midtown with a big flight deck 72 m up: three pads, a hangar, a fuel farm, a crane, a control tower and guns on every corner. The helicopters you own but aren't flying sit parked on the deck and in the hangar.

**Day and night**: a day lasts 12 minutes. At night it's properly dark: stars, a moon, the city's windows and neon lit up, more zombies and faster ones, and kills pay 50% more. Fit a SEARCHLIGHT (L).

**Zombies**: just infected people, but thousands of them (up to ~4,600 around you; the city streets are packed, the countryside much less). Walkers, runners, crawlers (can't climb), screamers (they shriek and the whole horde comes), BRUTES (huge, tough, can't be knocked over, heavy on the skids), BLOATERS (burst when they die and take the ones round them too) and RIOT POLICE (helmets and vests shrug off bullets: use explosives or armour-piercing). They can't fly or spit: they climb on top of each other. A pile builds up against a wall until the top ones reach the roof, and a heap forms under you if you hover low and slow. Spikes on the skids (ANTI-CLIMB) make them let go.

**The story**: seven chapters from Spire Control, told over the radio, each with goals that tick off as you play (shown top left and on THE SPIRE screen in the hangar): FIRST LIGHT (rescues and a crate), EYES AND EARS (land an engineer at each radio mast and keep the dead off the hut; working masts let you hear calls from further away), FORT CALDER (clear out the garrison: then it's a second base with fuel, repairs and guns), THE LONG ROAD (convoys and a horde), ST MARY'S (evacuate 30 people off the hospital roof while the whole district comes), THE SIEGE (six waves come for the Spire's doors: if they break in, the Spire loses power for two minutes) and THE GREAT HORDE (1,500 of them walking on the Borough). Then free flight.

**The Spire** (🏢 THE SPIRE in the hangar): everyone you rescue moves in. People with a bed go out scavenging and bring in scrap every minute you fly. Build BUNKS (more beds), DEFENCE GUNS (twin .50s → Bofors 40 mm → Phalanx CIWS), a FUEL DEPOT (faster refuelling, cheaper fuel drops, then instant service), a WORKSHOP (cheaper upgrades and helicopters), a CLINIC (tougher team, free recruits) and a RADAR ROOM (more jobs at once). Each one needs scrap and enough people living there.

**Your team**: TEAM (T) at the Spire. Recruit people (name them, pick a job: rifleman, medic, scavenger, breacher, scout), buy guns, armour, medkits, grenades and backpacks in the armoury and hand them out. Land on a building with a lit sign (pharmacy, police, gas station, hospital, gun shop…) and press R to send whoever's on board in, from the roof or the street door. The tracker shows them clearing it floor by floor; tell them to meet you on the ROOF (Y) or the STREET (U), or PULL OUT (K). Pick them up (E) and they bring back scrap, fuel, gear and survivors. Bitten team members don't come back.

**Events**: RESCUE (people on a roof under a green flare, with a pile growing up the wall), CRASH SITE (a helicopter down on a roof, the crew alive by the burning wreck), BRIDGE (a crowd stuck in the traffic on a bridge, the dead walking in from both ends), OUTBREAK (a town square packed with them: clear nine in ten before the clock runs out), CONVOY (survivors' trucks driving to safety: they only move while you're overhead, and stop when the road ahead is blocked), HORDE (hundreds of them walking across the country towards a named town) and SUPPLY DROP (hook it on and take it to the Spire).

**Helicopters** (HELICOPTER in the hangar, each with a smooth lofted body made in `hulls.js`): WARHORSE (the all-rounder, fits everything), HUEY (the classic: cheap, big open doors, a two-blade rotor, 6 to 14 seats), SEA KING (a boat-hulled rescue helicopter that can land on water, with a rescue hoist fitted and 14 to 26 seats), OSPREY (a tiltrotor: the wingtip nacelles tip forward as it speeds up; very fast, 16 to 28 seats), HARRIER (a jump jet on four swivelling nozzles: the fastest of all, one seat), ALLIGATOR (a heavy attack helicopter with stacked coaxial rotors and no tail rotor), HORNET (a tiny fast scout with benches outside), VIPER (a slim two-seat attack helicopter, the fastest of all), REAPER (a gunship: tandem cockpit, chin gun +30%, +50% rockets, heavy armour), KESTREL (a big three-engine utility helicopter, 12 to 24 seats, fits nearly everything) and MULE (a twin-rotor transport: 16 to 40 seats, huge fuel, very tough, slow, no rockets). Upgrades are bought once and fit any helicopter they're not too big for.

**Planes**: WARTHOG (a straight-wing attack plane built round a 30 mm cannon) and SPECTRE (a four-engine gunship with guns out of the left side) take off from THE AIRFIELD (a 1.3 km runway with an apron, hangars, a control tower and fuel, out in the flat country) and land there to refuel; HARRIER and OSPREY hover like helicopters and switch to flying like planes with V. Planes fly differently: W / S set the throttle, the mouse pitches and banks, A / D is the rudder, the wings only lift with speed and below the stall speed the nose drops. They have their own upgrades (FLAPS, AIRBRAKES / thrust reversers, AFTERBURNER, ROUGH-FIELD GEAR, WING HARDPOINTS) and helicopters keep theirs (rotor, winch, skids, hover hold, marksman, medical bay); the hangar only shows the ones that fit. Planes can't land on roofs, so near an SOS or a supply drop they press E to call a DUSTOFF (an AI rescue helicopter from the Spire, up to three at once) and keep the zombies off while it loads; it won't load while zombies are on the roof, and goes back for anyone left. Over a radio mast, E drops the engineer by parachute.

**Customise it**: PAINT JOB (23 of them, from olive and navy grey to police, medevac, coastguard and solid gold), CAMO (woodland, desert, urban, tiger stripes, splinter, digital, arctic, jungle, lizard), NOSE ART (shark mouth, flames, skull and crossbones, chequered nose, lightning bolt, sunburst), TAIL MARKINGS (invasion stripes, rescue bands, your own tail number, a chequered band, a star roundel), TRIM COLOUR (a second colour over the paint job's own), WINDOW TINT (smoked, gold visor, blue, NVG green, amber) and ROTOR TIPS (yellow, white, red, dayglo, black or striped).

**Upgrades** (drones, noise beacons, flashbangs and the loudspeaker were taken out and refunded; the nose gun and the wing pods are drawn smaller so they don't swamp the helicopter; each tier changes how the helicopter looks; tiers go grey → green → blue → purple → orange): nose gun (chain gun up to TWIN 30MM), rockets (up to APKWS guided pods), bombs (up to THERMOBARIC), armour (up to CERAMIC COMPOSITE), engine (EMERGENCY POWER boost on SHIFT, then UPRATED ENGINES), rotor (up to COMPOSITE BLADES), door guns (gunners, miniguns, then MK19 grenade launchers), fuel tanks, cabin, winch (up to a RESCUE HOIST), thermal camera, anti-climb skids, searchlight (L, then NIGHTSUN), medical bay (up to a TRAUMA BAY), supply chutes (B), radio (up to SATCOM), NIGHT VISION (Z), AMMO (armour-piercing or incendiary), REPAIRS (field repair kit, crew chief), FIRE SUPPRESSION (survive one killing hit a sortie), BARREL COOLING (cooled, then quick-change barrels), FLIGHT COMPUTER (HOVER HOLD: let go and it stops dead; then a ground proximity warning), GUN SIGHT (stabilised, then a helmet sight), QUIET KIT (zombies hear you from less far), TARGETING POD (laser-guided rockets, then a long-range camera that maps every zombie within 900 m), the GAU-19 .50 gatling as a sixth nose gun, CRASH SAFETY (crashworthy seats save the survivors aboard if you're shot down; then crash-resistant fuel: no fireball, no repair bill), WEAPON RACKS, GUN PODS (.50 cal, then 20 mm cannon pods on the wings that fire with the nose gun) and a MARKSMAN (a sharpshooter in the cabin who picks off screamers, riot police and anything on the skids, then an anti-materiel rifle that drops brutes). The belly turret, tail gun and electric skids were taken out; anything spent on them was given back.

**Fuel**: runs down as you fly. Refuel at the Spire, bring it back from gas stations, send passengers out for it (Q) or pay for a fuel drop (G).

**Online**: 🌐 ONLINE in the hangar → MAKE A SERVER: give it a name and make it public (it shows in everyone's server list) or private (code only). Friends press JOIN A FRIEND on the title screen, pick FLY MY OWN HELICOPTER (up to 3 wingmen, each in their own helicopter with their own upgrades, earning their own scrap) or RIDE IN THE DOOR (up to 2 gunners), then click a server in the list or type its code. The host's game runs the zombies and the story; a wingman's game flies their helicopter and sends over what it hit. Wingmen can pick people up off roofs and drop them at the Spire. `?net=local` tests it in two tabs.

Code: `src/heli/`: `game.js` (hangar, flying, landing, raids, HUD, the friend's view), `terrain.js` (the procedural land, streamed in chunks), `trees.js` (the forests), `map.js` (city, towns, the Spire), `horde.js` (zombies and piles), `team.js` (team, gear, raids), `events.js` (including the story jobs), `story.js` (the chapters), `chopper.js` (model and flight), `hulls.js` (the smooth helicopter bodies), `coop.js` (online), `data.js` (upgrades, save), `ui.js` (styles). The renderer and helpers in `src/` come from The Semi-Finals.

`mech.html` is the earlier mech sketch.
